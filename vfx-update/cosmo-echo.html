Cosmos Echo

A 5-minute VR experience about a dying astronaut who finds echoes of his life in the stars.

Watch the video ↗

The experience

You are an astronaut drifting alone in space with five minutes of oxygen left. Through a telescope, you find echoes of your life in the stars: Saturn's rings hum the song from your wedding, and a passing comet brings back your daughter's kite. As each breath fades, love becomes your last connection to the world.

My role

I was a 3D artist and technical artist on the team.

  • Made the hologram planet shader in Shader Graph
  • Modeled the hologram projector and other props
  • Drew the pixel art memory scenes
  • Made visual effects for atmosphere, scale and memory
