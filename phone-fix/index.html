About

Hi, I'm Frank.

I'm a student at the Entertainment Technology Center (ETC) at Carnegie Mellon University, where I also work part-time as a 3D generalist and as the ETC's Social Media Assistant. I model, rig, animate and edit, so I can take a project from the first sketch to the final post.

Before CMU, I studied Film and Media Arts at the University of Utah, ran Instagram for the Student Philanthropy and Engagement Center, and built CG models for planetarium software at Cosm, Evans & Sutherland.

Tools: Blender, Maya, ZBrush, Substance, Unity, Unreal, Premiere Pro, After Effects
